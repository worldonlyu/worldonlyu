# Why I started

*Write this entry yourself, in your own words, and date it. Delete this paragraph when you do. The chapters can tell you what is known; only you can record why you wanted to know it, and in a year that record will show you how far you have come.*

Some prompts, if they help:

- Who the cat was, and what the six years were like.
- What you hope to understand, stated as plainly as you can.
- What you already believe about whether a mind can be preserved or transferred, and how confident you are.
- What would change your mind, in either direction.
- What you want this project to be a year from now, whatever the science says.
